MILES HIGH CLUB

Miles Elliot (Daddy Campbell) won the Miles Bowl and took sole possession of first place with a 147.26-133.96 victory over Miles Blue (blueballs). Miles E's wonderboy Jahmyr Gibbs put up 37.70pts, but the highest-scoring player of the week belonged to the loser as Drake Maye gave Blue an insufficient 39.46pts.

This is only the sixth time in Waxball history that a manager started the week's highest-scoring player and still lost. Mosko was the first with Lamar Jackson's 34.44pts in his Week 7 2024 loss to Trav. Sam has achieved this thrice, with Bijan Robinson's 35.80pts in Week 6 2025, Josh Allen's 37.84pts in Week 14 2025 and Caleb Williams' 37.26pts in Week 1 of this year. Blue owns the other two: Derrick Henry's 45.60pts in the 2025 championship, a game he lost to Milo by 0.80pts, and now Maye. Somehow, half of all six belong to Sam.

The result leaves Miles E as the league's only 4-1 manager and gives him a league-best 684.96 PF. His four-game streak is already tied for the third-longest in Waxball history. Blue scored the fifth-most points of Week 5, becoming the week's highest-scoring loser and slipped from 2nd to 3rd. This matchup tied the Miles Bowl all-time series 3-3 and pushed its Rivalry Score from 80 to 90/100.
