CHAMPIONSHIP BLUES FADE

After suffering one of the closest losses of 2025 in last year’s championship, Blue (blueballs; 145.36pts) dominated Milo (Nacua Matata; 100.70pts) by the week’s largest win margin in our Heated Rivalry of the week. JSN was almost entirely responsible, as he put up 42.50pts on his own, 95.1% of the spread and 29.2% of Blue’s entire weekly score. The victory is Blue’s first ever over Manheim after going 0-2 in Milo’s rookie season, one being the tight championship matchup and the other being a 42.90pts thrashing by Milo, making the all-time point record between these two almost exactly even (Milo 396.04pts-397.00pts Blue).

This is Blue’s 3rd weekly highest score ever and extends his league-leading weekly top-three finishes to 15. Erik (Pamela Mari Ohno Dagoberg; 143.26pts) is hot on his heels, also finishing on the podium this week and pushing his total top-three finishes to 14.

Milo’s active four-game win streak, including last year’s playoffs, comes to an end with his lowest score since his last loss in Week 14 of 2025 (77.18pts v Erik).
