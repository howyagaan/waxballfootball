At 8-1, Miles now owns the best record any Waxball manager who started 0-1 has had after Week 9. The next best was 6-3, reached by Erik in 2024 and Mosko in 2025.

BLUE IT

Trav (darryluvr3000) beat Blue (blueballs) 127.38-75.36, stretching his series lead to 3-1 and turning the league's hottest manager into the week's second-lowest scorer.

Blue entered Week 9 after consecutive weekly highs of 149.04pts and 145.06pts. He fell 69.70pts from one week to the next, the sixth-largest scoring drop in Waxball history and the largest since Trav in Week 7, finishing only 0.22pts above his career low of 75.14pts.

Havi climbs to second place alone at 6-3, two games behind the league lead. His opponent Milo has the largest drop of the week (-3), falling to 7th place. With Puka likely out on IR, he may struggle in the stretch towards the playoffs.

STAYIN' ALIVE

Nine weeks down and nobody has secured a playoff spot. Nobody has been eliminated either. Miles sits three games clear in first and Mosko sits last, but all 12 managers remain mathematically capable of reaching the playoffs or falling into the Toilet Bowl.

It took until Week 11 for the first playoff elimination of 2024, when Will was knocked out of contention. The first berths that season were secured one week later by Sam and Miles E after Week 12.

In 2025, both took until Week 12: Paul became the first manager to secure a playoff place, while Miles E became the first eliminated.

Miles E looks to keep extending his record streak as he puts eight straight on the line against the only manager left in Waxball he has never beaten. Miles and Erik are first and second in PF so far this season, though Erik sits in 6th place at 4-5.

Havi has never beaten Blue in two matchups; Blue's career-high 167.76pts came in this series in Week 8 of 2024.

After Mosko's weekly low in Week 9, Mosko and Will now account for 6 of the 10 lowest scores in Waxball history. They currently sit in 11th- and 12th-place.

Trav and Paul enter tied 1-1 after Trav produced Week 12 2025's highest score of 150.22pts in their last meeting. They meet with Paul at 3-6 and Trav at 4-5, although Paul holds a higher PF.

The week's Heated Rivalry belongs to Christian and Sam. Their series is tied 2-2. Christian knocked 2024 regular-season winner Sam out of the playoffs before winning that year's title. After three meetings in 2024 alone, their only 2025 game was a 56.30pt Christian blowout.
