INCHES FROM HISTORY

Christian (Steeler Virginity) beat Sam (mistahbigdick) 173.94-89.12 in the week's Heated Rivalry. The second-largest blowout in Waxball history fell just 0.36pts short of Erik's record 85.18pt demolition of Trav in Week 10 last year.

The score is also a new career high for Christian, beating his previous best of 162.68pts, and the seventh-highest score Waxball has ever seen. It gives him his sixth career weekly high, while the result is the heaviest defeat of Sam's career.

Christian's +4 jump from 9th to 5th is the biggest climb of the week. The performance also moves him past Erik into first place in all-time point differential, +261.66 to Erik's +259.98, and into second place in all-time PF. A monumental week for 2024's champ. At the same point that season, Christian was 5-5 in 6th before finishing the regular season 8-6 in 4th on his way to the championship.

PAPA'S PUNCHED PLAYOFF TICKET

Miles E (Daddy Campbell) beat Erik (Pamela Mari Ohno Dagoberg) for the first time 167.76-125.24, extending the longest regular-season winning streak in Waxball history to nine. Miles becomes the fifth manager to beat every other manager at least once, joining Havi, Blue, Milo and Nic.

At 9-1, Miles has mathematically secured a playoff place earlier than anyone in Waxball history. Even if he loses out and finishes 9-5, the five current 5-5 managers cannot all reach nine wins, as they all play each other before season's end. At most three can join Miles on nine wins, so even if Havi passes him, Commish can finish no lower than 5th when six qualify. Commish will now be competing for a first-week playoff bye.

Miles' 1,353.04pts are the most anyone has scored through Week 10, beating Paul's 2025 mark of 1,300.30pts. Maintain this pace and Miles would finish on 1,894.26pts, narrowly passing Erik's full regular-season record of 1,890.76pts in 2024.

Erik remains 8th at 4-6 despite ranking second in both 2026 PF and All-Play. More on this in the Week 11 preview.

JAVIER CLIMBING

Havi (Papi Coop) beat Blue (blueballs) for the first time 133.56-85.22, putting Blue in Trav and Mosko's position earlier this season: setting a four-week swing of weekly high, weekly high, second-low and weekly low. Havi has won four straight, the longest active streak outside Miles' record run, and now occupies second place alone at 7-3.

Everyone else remains packed beneath Havi and Commish. Five managers are 5-5 and another four are 4-6, leaving only two games between 3rd and 11th. Will and Mosko both climbed out of the bottom two with wins, while Paul lost a second straight game and dropped beneath them into last place at 3-7.

Paul in 12th holds a 99.98pt PF lead on 11th-place Mosko, and a 44-66 All-Play record compared to Mosko's 33-77. Erik and Milo both have winning All-Play records (67-43, 57-53) and PF totals higher than most of the league, yet sit just outside the playoff picture.
